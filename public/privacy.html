Privacy Policy - Farewell Photos

Farewell Photos is a small private website where students with a college Google account share farewell photos and find the photos they appear in.

What we collect

  • Your Google account email and name, to log you in.
  • Photos you upload, together with your name and college email, so that people who download your photos can contact you.
  • Numeric face signatures calculated in your browser from uploaded photos and from your live selfie. The selfie image itself is never saved.

How it is used

Only to log you in and to show you the photos you appear in. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else. Data is stored with Supabase.

Deletion

All data will be deleted after the event. To have your data removed earlier, email kaarmukilanag17@gmail.com.
